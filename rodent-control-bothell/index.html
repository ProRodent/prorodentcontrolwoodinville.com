Streets, lots and rodent routes.

Because the Bothell mailing area crosses a county line, it covers a wide range of settings, from older streets near the center to newer subdivisions, wooded lots and commercial corridors. Wet Pacific Northwest weather sends rodents indoors. When autumn turns cold and rainy, mice and rats look for warm, dry spaces, and a gap that went unused all summer suddenly becomes an entrance. Calls tend to rise with the first sustained rain.

Garages and crawl spaces are the quiet back doors of most homes. Door seals flatten, vent screens tear, and stored boxes provide nest material. Those spaces are often unheated and rarely checked, so a problem can run for weeks before anyone notices.
