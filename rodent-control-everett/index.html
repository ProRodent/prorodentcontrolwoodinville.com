Housing and setting.

Everett sits on Port Gardner Bay and the Snohomish River, with a working waterfront, an older downtown, residential hills and large retail and industrial areas. Port and industrial districts have warehouses and docks. Residential hills have older homes and newer subdivisions with their own mix of gaps.

Wet Pacific Northwest weather sends rodents indoors. When autumn turns cold and rainy, mice and rats look for warm, dry spaces, and a gap that went unused all summer suddenly becomes an entrance. Calls tend to rise with the first sustained rain.

Yards matter as much as houses. Bird feeders, compost, chicken feed, fruit that drops and woodpiles stacked against a wall all feed and shelter rodents. Changing a few of those habits often cuts activity before any trap is set.
