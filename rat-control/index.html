Two kinds of rat in the Puget Sound area.

Norway rats and roof rats are both found in the Puget Sound region, and they behave differently. Norway rats are heavy, stay low and dig. They favor burrows along foundations, crawl spaces, compost piles and the edges of creeks and drainage ditches. Roof rats are slimmer and climb. They use overhanging branches, utility lines and fence tops to reach roofs and attics.

The difference matters because the plan changes. A Norway rat problem is usually solved at ground level with trapping near burrows and sealing at the foundation. A roof rat problem needs work at the roofline, in the attic and in the tree canopy close to the house.

Why rats show up around Woodinville.

Woodinville sits in a river valley with creeks, greenbelts and wooded hillsides all close to many homes. Those are travel corridors for rats. Add large lots, gardens, chicken coops, bird feeders and compost bins, and food and cover are close at hand.

Commercial areas bring their own draw. Restaurants, tasting rooms and garden retailers move food, cardboard and soil through delivery doors every day, and a dumpster enclosure with a gap at the base is an open invitation.

  • Burrows along foundations, fences, sheds and under decks.
  • Droppings about half an inch long in attics, garages or crawl spaces.
  • Gnawed fruit, nuts or chicken feed.
  • Heavy thumping or running overhead at dusk or just after dark.
  • Greasy rub marks along rafters, pipes and wall edges.

How we remove rats.

Rats are cautious animals. They notice new objects and avoid them for days, so trap placement and patience matter more than the number of traps. We place traps along confirmed runs, often inside protective stations that keep pets and children out, and we check them on a set schedule instead of leaving them for weeks.

Bait is used carefully, if at all. Poisoned rats frequently crawl into walls or ceilings and die there, and the smell lasts a long time. Federal rules also restrict which rodenticides homeowners can buy, which is one reason the plan leans on trapping. Our trapping and removal page explains the details.

Closing the way back in.

Rats chew through soft materials, so sealing uses metal, hardware cloth and masonry-grade patching. Half an inch is enough for a rat to squeeze through, and the common openings are crawl space vents, gaps around utility lines, damaged soffits and worn door sweeps. See rodent exclusion for the full list.

Outdoor changes help as much as indoor ones. Trim limbs back from the roof, store pet and chicken feed in sealed metal bins, and keep firewood and debris off the ground against the house. Our backyard and outdoor control page covers the yard side in more detail.

What follow-up looks like.

Rat jobs usually run longer than mouse jobs, because rats are warier and often have more than one nest. Expect a first visit for inspection and trap placement, return visits to check and reset, and sealing once catches stop. If a nest has soiled the attic, cleanup comes after removal, covered on our attic and crawl space cleanup page.

Before we arrive

  • Keep children and pets out of rooms with droppings, nests or open gaps until we have looked.
  • Leave dry droppings alone, because sweeping them raises dust you do not want to breathe.
  • Skip loose bait or poison, which complicates the inspection and can leave animals dying in walls.
  • Jot down the hours when you hear or see activity, since timing helps trace the route.

Questions about rat control

Are the rats in my house Norway or roof rats?

Burrows and noises low in the house suggest Norway rats, while heavy movement overhead and nibbled fruit up high suggest roof rats. We confirm on the inspection.

Is it safe to have rats near chickens or pets?

Rats draw to feed and eggs. Tell us about every animal on the property so traps are placed where pets cannot reach them.

Why not just use poison?

Poisoned rats often die inside walls, which leads to odor and flies. Consumer rodenticides are also restricted. Trapping on confirmed routes is cleaner and easier to track.

How long until the rats are gone?

It varies with how many animals and nests there are. We estimate visits after the inspection and tell you if the plan changes.
