What changes with the weather.

Holiday storage brings its own risk. Cardboard boxes of decorations carried into a garage or attic in winter, then left for months, make ideal nests. Moving them into lidded totes and keeping them off the floor cuts the risk.

Housing and setting.

Medina is largely residential, with large lots, mature landscaping and homes set among trees and gardens near the water. Established landscaping gives rodents cover, and large houses have large roofs, garages and outbuildings to inspect.

Garages and crawl spaces are the quiet back doors of most homes. Door seals flatten, vent screens tear, and stored boxes provide nest material. Those spaces are often unheated and rarely checked, so a problem can run for weeks before anyone notices.

Trees and landscaping do a lot of the work for rodents. Branches that touch the roof become bridges, and dense plantings against the foundation give cover right at the wall. A short walk around the outside usually shows which plants and limbs matter.
