Coverage overview

Our home base is Woodinville, ZIP 98077. Around it we cover 18 nearby towns and communities that appear in the coverage data and sit within about 15 miles by ZIP-code distance. Each was checked against the coverage list and a separate distance calculation, so the ZIP and distance on each page are not guessed from a map. Find your town below, confirm that your ZIP matches, and call if it does not.

Not every town is the same kind of place, and we say so. Most are incorporated cities. Bothell is a mailing area that crosses a county line, with one ZIP in King County and two in Snohomish County. Fall City is an unincorporated community with no town government. Seattle, Everett and Bellevue are large, so we list only the ZIP codes that fall inside our range.

Snohomish County towns are in a different county from Woodinville, which is in King County. That does not change how we inspect a house. It does change which county office handles permits for work such as sealing and repair, so we mention it where it matters.

Before you call, have three things ready: your street address with its ZIP, what you saw or heard and when it started, and whether your building is a house, a shared building or a business. Your ZIP decides which page applies. If your mail says 98072 instead of 98077, or your ZIP is not listed, say so on the call and we confirm coverage before booking anything.

Questions about our coverage area

Do you cover every ZIP code in a large city like Seattle?

No. Only the ZIP codes that sit within about 15 miles of Woodinville are listed on each city page. Seattle, Everett and Bellevue each show exactly which ones.

Is Bothell inside King County or Snohomish County?

Both. The city of Bothell straddles the county line. One Bothell ZIP is in King County and two are in Snohomish County, and the Bothell page explains the split.

Why are neighborhoods listed separately from towns?

Neighborhood pages describe parts of Woodinville itself. Town pages describe separate nearby places with their own ZIP codes and governments.

What if my town is not listed?

Call and ask. If the address is outside our confirmed range, we will say so on the call instead of promising a visit we cannot make.
