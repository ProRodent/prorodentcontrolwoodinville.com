What we do.

We handle mouse and rat problems for homes and businesses in Woodinville and the nearby towns on our areas page. Every visit follows the same order. Inspect the property to find where activity comes from, treat with trapping or sealing matched to what was found, and close the entry points so the problem does not return.

That order matters more than any single product. A trap set without knowing the travel route rarely works, and a repair done without finding every gap just leaves another one open.

How we explain a job.

You hear the plan and the price before any work starts, based on what the inspection found rather than a guess over the phone. If chewed wiring, insulation or ducts turn up, that damage is documented and priced as its own line item.

We do not publish reviews, star ratings or credential badges on this site, because we would rather show you how a visit works than ask you to trust a number. This site does not display license, permit or insurance details, so if a document matters for your job, ask about it on the call before you book.

Availability.

The line is answered 24 hours a day, and same-day visits are offered where the schedule allows. Whether the call is about scratching at midnight or a routine inspection next week, the same inspect, treat and seal approach applies.

What you can expect on a visit.

Expect a technician who looks before talking. The walk starts at the roofline and the exterior, moves to the attic, crawl space, garage and kitchen, and ends with a plain explanation. You will hear which animal is involved, where it gets in and what the options are, with a price for each part.

Expect honest limits, too. Some noises are not rodents, and some problems are minor. If a visit is not needed, we say so on the phone. If it is, we tell you what to do in the meantime, such as keeping pets away from droppings and not sweeping them dry.

Expect a follow-up. Trapping is checked on a schedule, and sealing is confirmed on a return visit. A job is not finished when the traps go down. It is finished when activity stops and the entry points hold.

Why we inspect before anything else.

A technician who skips the inspection and goes straight to traps or a price is guessing. Every house has its own mix of roof type, age and landscaping, and that mix decides where rodents get in. We would rather spend the time up front than return three times because the first guess missed the real gap.

The scale changes between a quick mouse call and a commercial account. The order of work does not change, and neither does the follow-up visit.

What this site is and is not.

This site is a local phone line and information resource for rodent control. It is not a walk-in office, and the address in the footer is for mailing and citation only. We describe how the work is done, what it involves and what to do while you wait.

We also try to be plain about what we do not know. We cannot tell you from a distance whether a noise is a mouse, a rat or a loose pipe, which is why the first visit is an inspection and not a promise.
